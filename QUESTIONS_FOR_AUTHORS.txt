NEW Q6 — how should the injection-layer question be resolved?
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

You asked why the injection grid stops at layer 15 of 32. The paper does not
say, and no experiment in the repo swept the injection layer.

What we found instead: the base->ck8 displacement norm grows ~5000x with depth
(GradDiff: 0.04 at L1, 5.08 at L15, 222.2 at L32). So at alpha=1 the TOTAL
injected norm is fixed 9.5, late 67.4 (7.1x), all-32 463.7 (48.8x), and layer 32
alone is 48% of the all-32 budget. The all-vs-fixed comparison I first reported
was therefore confounded by magnitude, not position -- your norm-matching
suggestion is the correct fix and is now implemented (modes allnorm/latenorm).
